04 · The path, in detail

AI prepares the choice. People own the consequence.

Adviser decision

Approve, amend or reject. A reason is recorded and amendments require replanning.

→

One approved action

The weekly package and learner view contain only the action the adviser approved.

→

Feedback with a way back

Unresolved, correction and human-help responses reopen review without erasing history.

Capacity matters twice: before approval, so the plan is deliverable; after feedback, so unmet demand remains visible until the team can respond.
