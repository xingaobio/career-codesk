## Authority and reading guide

This is a candidate RFC, not implementation authority. Its recommendations are defaults for human review only; `G001` must approve or amend them before substantive product implementation begins. `SPEC.md` supplies engineering-loop context, not Career CoDesk product authority.

The preserved [v0.1.0 early product concept](v0.1.0-early-product-concept.md) remains historical evidence. It is not rewritten or relabelled as approved. Claims about markets, regulation, competitors, commercial value, and scenario scale remain dated assumptions to validate, not compliance advice or promises.

## Source evidence

- The 0.1 snapshot defines the proposed problem as turning learner needs into a capacity-feasible, adviser-approved intervention plan, with explicit unmet demand rather than outcome guarantees (0.1, sections 1 and 5).
- It separates source information, provisional AI interpretation, adviser decisions, intervention allocation, delivery, outcome confirmation, and writeback records (0.1, section 3.1).
- It requires a safety or serious-welfare disclosure to leave the ordinary career workflow; it must not become a career-risk score or ordinary queue item (0.1, section 3.2).
- It assigns arithmetic, entitlement, hard constraints, and schedule feasibility to deterministic code, while AI may interpret and propose subject to meaningful adviser review (0.1, section 4.1).
- It frames a modular monolith with relational storage and a background queue as sufficient for an MVP, while leaving the first college, MIS, writeback target, entitlement, and other direction-setting choices open (0.1, sections 4 and 8).
- The versioning rules require separate evidence, assumptions, decisions, and open questions, and require explicit approval status ([versions README](README.md)).

## Assumptions requiring validation

- A careers team will find a provenance-preserving, capacity-aware decision queue more useful than an additional generic learner chatbot or content catalogue.
- A synthetic, local demonstration can test workflow clarity and hard-constraint handling before a design partner, live MIS, or real learner data is introduced.
- A small, finite need taxonomy is sufficient to demonstrate grouping and proposed interventions without clinical, welfare, SEND, or predictive classification.
- The sample policies, capacity values, resource link, service catalogue, and learner feedback are demo fixtures, not institutional policy, legal entitlement, safeguarding process, or provider claims.
- Market observations and the proposed whitespace in the 0.1 snapshot were current only as recorded on 2026-07-17 and need fresh primary-source research before commercial use.

## Candidate recommendations

### Prototype boundary

The prototype **MUST** operate only on deterministic, obviously synthetic learner fixtures and CSV/mock inputs. It **MUST** use synthetic identifiers, a field allowlist, and a synthetic-data attestation at import. Every displayed source is labelled `Synthetic mock source — no MIS connection`.

It is in scope to import a local CSV, preserve its provenance, produce a transparent hypothesis with a deterministic fake AI adapter, calculate a weekly capacity proposal, obtain a staff decision, show a learner’s single approved next action, accept simulated feedback, and generate a local mock export record.

It is out of scope to use real learner PII (including merely pseudonymised or mapped records), SEND, medical, safeguarding, or welfare records; connect to a live MIS; send email, calendar, payment, or provider requests; write to any external system; deploy; use network-dependent demo behaviour; or make any other external mutation. A mock export is a local, versioned record only and must not imply vendor integration, synchronisation, interoperability, or approval.

The slice does not replace Level 6 personal guidance, diagnose a learner, predict NEET status, or claim guaranteed Gatsby, Ofsted, statutory, or legal compliance. It does not contain a broad chatbot, proprietary careers content library, provider marketplace, or learner-facing generative AI.

### Primary manager/adviser workflow

The primary surface is a manager/adviser decision queue and weekly planning workbench, not a general analytics dashboard.

1. A staff member imports an allowlisted synthetic CSV and records its source, schema version, fixture version, import time, and attestation.
2. Intake preserves each source statement as a `NeedCapture`. The AI gateway produces a separately stored, provisional `NeedHypothesis`, including tags, explanation, confidence/unknowns, policy/prompt/model or fake-adapter version, and input record IDs.
3. Before ordinary interpretation or prioritisation, the sensitive-data gate detects a safety or safeguarding-like signal. It creates a restricted `SafetyExit`, stops ordinary processing, and gives no scoring, routing, schedule, or learner-facing recommendation.
4. The planning module groups eligible proposed needs and deterministically tests policy entitlement, deadlines, capacity, group limits, conflicts, and tie-breaks. It records feasible allocations and unmet demand; it does not silently drop demand when capacity is exhausted.
5. The queue presents source evidence, provisional hypothesis, unknowns, alternative feasible routes, capacity effect, and unmet-demand consequence together. An adviser meaningfully approves, amends, or rejects the proposal and records the reason.
6. Only an approved decision can create an active allocation and an executable weekly plan. The system may then create a local mock export attempt for the approved record.
7. The learner view shows only the approved next action: one mock approved-resource link or agreed action, why it was selected, estimated effort, deadline, and controls to request human help or correct source information.
8. The learner can mark the action `helped` or `unresolved`. `unresolved`, a human-help request, or a correction request records feedback and reopens the case for staff review; it never overwrites prior evidence or decisions.

### Minimum learner feedback interaction

For this prototype, the learner interaction is a local simulated view of one approved action. It has no conversational AI. It must make the action, reason, effort, deadline, human-help request, and correction route clear. A correction creates a new capture linked to the superseded source; it does not edit the original. An unresolved response creates a new open review state and preserves the delivery and outcome history.

## Canonical records, authority, and provenance

All records use stable IDs, `created_at`, actor/type, schema version, and tenant/demo-fixture scope. References are IDs, not copied mutable descriptions. The prototype has one shared relational store; no UI or module owns a second learner profile.

| Record | Purpose and authoritative writer | Required provenance / rules |
|---|---|---|
| `Learner` | Synthetic person identity; intake owns fixture identity. | Synthetic identifier and fixture version only; never a real person or mapped identifier. |
| `Enrolment` | Synthetic course/cohort context; intake owns imported context. | Links to `Learner`, source row, source version, and import. |
| `Need` | Versioned taxonomy concept; policy/configuration owns it. | Taxonomy version and permitted route definitions; it is not a diagnosis. |
| `Case` | Operational container for one learner and active support episode; casework owns state. | Links learner, enrolment where relevant, and all related evidence; concurrent cases remain possible. |
| `NeedCapture` | Verbatim structured/free-text source supplied by mock CSV, learner, or adviser. | Immutable source payload/row, source type, import/capture time, source version, field allowlist result, and correction/supersession link. |
| `NeedHypothesis` | Provisional machine interpretation of one or more captures. | Input capture IDs, output schema, tags, explanation, confidence/unknowns, gateway policy and model/fake version; never overwrites a capture. |
| `SupportDecision` | Adviser approval, amendment, or rejection of a proposal. | Immutable decision event with adviser identity, reason, reviewed inputs, policy/planner version, and predecessor decision. Only an adviser writes it. |
| `InterventionAllocation` | Proposed or approved use of a service, slot, or resource. | Links proposal inputs and deterministic planner run; active only when backed by an approved decision. |
| `DeliveryEvent` | Simulated delivery/attendance/completion record. | Actor/time, allocation ID, result and source; distinct from a plan or outcome. |
| `OutcomeConfirmation` | Learner/adviser response that an intervention helped, remains unresolved, or is otherwise confirmed. | Actor/time, linked delivery/action and exact response; unresolved reopens rather than edits history. |
| `WritebackAttempt` | Local mock-export audit event. | Approved decision/allocation ID, idempotency key, payload version/digest, attempt number, result (`pending`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `not_sent`), and failure reason. `succeeded` means mock output persisted locally only. |
| `SafetyExit` | Restricted simulated handoff marker. | Minimal signal metadata, source ID, restricted-access marker, time, and human-handling status; no ordinary-case score, hypothesis, allocation, or export. |

### Record and correction invariants

- Source records, hypotheses, decisions, delivery events, confirmations, and export attempts remain distinct append-only evidence. A display may derive a current view but cannot erase history.
- A hypothesis always identifies its exact inputs and producing version. A decision identifies the hypothesis/capture versions actually reviewed. New interpretation produces a new hypothesis.
- Corrections are new source-qualified records with supersession links. Conflicting sources remain visible to staff; neither source silently becomes the truth.
- Adviser decisions are immutable events. Amendments and reversals create later decisions with reasons; they never rewrite an approval in place.
- The restricted safety store/path is access-separated from ordinary casework. In a demo it is only a simulated restricted handoff, with no invented contacts, response times, permissions, or claim of safeguarding adequacy.
- Export is permitted only for an approved allocation. Idempotency is based on the approved decision/allocation and payload version. A failed attempt is visible and retryable locally; it does not imply an external retry.

## Deterministic planning responsibilities

The planner, not AI, owns entitlement checks, numeric arithmetic, dates/deadlines, adviser and room capacity, workshop/group limits, timetable conflicts, sequencing, tie-breaking, feasibility, and the calculation and display of unmet demand. It must emit a reproducible planner-run record with input IDs, policy version, algorithm version, seed where applicable, and explanations of exclusions.

AI (represented by a deterministic fake for the demo) may only propose transparent taxonomy tags, candidate intervention formats, and plain-language explanations. It cannot approve decisions, reserve capacity, calculate entitlement, resolve conflicts, create an active allocation, or export. Where no feasible allocation exists, the planner records unmet demand and retains the adviser disposition or human-help request for review.

## Recommended implementation architecture

Recommend a single Python/Django modular monolith with a server-rendered, accessible staff and learner UI; SQLite for the deterministic demo, with a documented PostgreSQL migration path; a shared relational persistence layer with transactions; a server-side AI gateway interface backed by a deterministic fake; and an in-process mock outbox. This is a recommendation, not an approved technical commitment.

| Module | Owns | Public responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Intake and provenance | imports, fixtures, `Learner`, `Enrolment`, `NeedCapture` | Validate allowlist and attestation; expose immutable source evidence. |
| Casework | `Case`, current case projection | Link evidence and manage state transitions without owning source truth. |
| AI gateway | `NeedHypothesis` production contract | Validate structured output and preserve input/policy/version metadata; fake adapter for demo. |
| Planning | policy fixtures, planner runs, proposed allocations | Deterministically calculate feasibility, alternatives, and unmet demand. |
| Decisions | `SupportDecision` | Render review context and enforce adviser approval/amend/reject gate. |
| Delivery and feedback | `DeliveryEvent`, `OutcomeConfirmation` | Record simulated delivery and reopen requests. |
| Export | `WritebackAttempt`, local mock payload | Create only approved, idempotent local mock exports and persist outcomes. |
| Audit and evaluation | append-only audit projection and scenario results | Make provenance, access boundary, and acceptance evidence inspectable. |

Modules communicate through explicit application services and shared transactions. A module may read another module’s published records but may not directly rewrite the other module’s authoritative records. This avoids microservices and duplicated profiles while keeping future extraction possible.

## Demo scenarios and acceptance measures

All fixtures, planner outputs, and fake AI outputs must be deterministic and synthetic.

| Scenario | Required observable result |
|---|---|
| Happy path | Source capture → hypothesis → approved allocation → weekly plan → local mock export → `helped` confirmation is fully linked. |
| Over capacity | Planner respects hard constraints, records unmet demand and alternatives, and cannot silently discard a human-help request or adviser disposition. |
| Malformed or non-synthetic import | Allowlist/attestation failure rejects import before case creation and reports why. |
| AI failure or invalid output | No hypothesis is treated as fact; the case remains reviewable with an explicit failure record. |
| Safety-like signal | Restricted `SafetyExit` occurs before ordinary classification; no ordinary queue, score, allocation, or export is created. |
| Adviser override | Amendment/rejection preserves proposal and reason; no allocation activates without the resulting approval. |
| Failed mock export | Failed attempt, payload digest, and retry state remain visible; no external mutation occurs. |
| Learner unresolved/correction | Feedback is recorded; case reopens with complete prior history and a correction creates a linked new capture. |

Binary prototype acceptance measures are: no network calls or secrets; repeatable fixture-to-output runs; zero hard-constraint violations; complete source-to-decision provenance; no approval bypass; safety never enters the ordinary queue; only approved records receive mock exports; successful reopen history; and keyboard/label/contrast accessibility smoke checks for the two views. These are implementation quality measures, not proof of learner value, commercial demand, regulatory compliance, or institutional effectiveness.
