# Changelog

This project uses versioned product snapshots during its exploratory phase. Changes describe decisions and shifts in product direction, not only software releases.

## [0.1.0-early] - 2026-07-17

### Added

- Preserved the original product definition and UK FE research materials in local Git version control.
- Captured the first coherent product thesis derived from the project materials and design discussion.
- Defined three role-specific product surfaces over one shared orchestration backend.
- Defined the initial learner-need-to-outcome state model and provenance boundaries.
- Recorded the initial backend, AI-gateway, MIS-integration, and service-provider responsibilities.
- Recorded the first UK competitor scan and the proposed market whitespace.
- Recorded an intentionally narrow MVP boundary and the major decisions still requiring validation.

### Important status note

This is an exploration baseline, not a promise of functionality, legal compliance, technical feasibility, or market demand. Market observations are time-stamped and must be revalidated before commercial use.
