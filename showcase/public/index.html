One inspectable path

From a learner signal to an approved action—and back when it did not help.

The workflow makes the scarce decision visible: who needs which support, what the service can actually deliver, and who takes responsibility for the final choice.

01 · SIGNAL

Intake

Allowed fields enter with a source snapshot and simulated identity.

02 · EVIDENCE

Interpret

Source statements remain separate from provisional AI hypotheses.

03 · REALITY

Plan capacity

Deterministic rules test slots, alternatives and unmet demand.

04 · HUMAN GATE

Decide

An adviser approves, amends or rejects with a recorded reason.

05 · ACTION

Deliver

Only the approved action enters the weekly execution package.

06 · LEARNING

Feedback

Helped, unresolved, correction and human-help signals remain visible.

Integration and privacy by boundary

Designed to sit beside a college MIS, not replace it.

The product interface isolates partner-specific field mapping from the decision workflow. A college can adopt the same core model while keeping its own identity, permissions, retention and governance controls.
